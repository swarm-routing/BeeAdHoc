# The v0.2.0 BeeAdHoc anchor

**Status: complete (2026-11-16). The anchor MISSES, 3 of 5 pause times.**
At every pause time BeeAdHoc's NRL is below AODV's. It is also below DSDV's
at 1, 30 and 100 s. At 500 and 1000 s, BeeAdHoc and DSDV are level: the
paired CI spans zero. The pre-registered rule needs both comparisons at
4 of the 5 pause times. Org rule 6 counts a comparator's numbers only after
its three gates, so BeeAdHoc's v0.2.0 ships with this written infeasibility
verdict, as the rule fixed before dispatch says. Its numbers are not
presented as a reproduction of the report. This is the pattern of AntHocNet's
`v270a` (MISS, 3 of 5, the same two pause times) and of ARA's v0.2.0.

The pre-registration is on #5:
[`bee-anchor-1`](https://github.com/swarm-routing/BeeAdHoc/issues/5#issuecomment-6841625121)
(maintainer decision 2026-11-13) and

- **Source.** [PG460] §7.2.5, Fig. 7.14: "BeeAdHoc sends the smallest number
  of control packet[s]", flat over pause time. The cell is AntHocNet's
  `v270a` (swarm-routing/AntHocNet#800).
- **Cell.** `routing-compare --scenario=paper` with 100 nodes, 3000 × 1000 m
  and 900 s. Traffic is 20 CBR flows at 4 packets/s (64-byte packets).
  Mobility is random waypoint at U[0, 10] m/s, with pauses of 1, 30, 100, 500
  and 1000 s. The radio is two-ray with a 250 m range, over UDP.
  `aodv,dsdv,beeadhoc` run in one invocation per seed. Each cell uses
  RngRun 1–20, split into four jobs of five.
- **Commits.**
  - BeeAdHoc `bc9cb7d9b296db01c3bd7cdb822bf3966c37e326`
  - swarm-core `111a67385fbcdaecff5efa49cbdd66b20a355a09` (v0.4.0)

    (3.48-optimized, `fb7596b`)
- **Rule (PRIMARY, `v270a`'s).** At each pause time, beeadhoc's NRL must be
  below AODV's and below DSDV's, paired by seed: the 95 % CI of
  (beeadhoc − other), n = 20, lies wholly below zero. **PASS** if this holds
  against both at ≥ 4 of the 5 pause times. Otherwise **MISS**.
- **Deviations from `v270a`.**
  - This repository's `core/`.

  - 64-byte packets and no DSR arm, as in `v270a`.

## Reading

- **The trend holds against AODV and fails against DSDV at low mobility.**
  BeeAdHoc's NRL stays flat over pause time, about 10–11 control packets per
  delivered packet. AODV's NRL is about twice that. DSDV's NRL falls as
  mobility falls, and at 500 and 1000 s it meets BeeAdHoc's. The same
  happened in `v270a` (NRL vs DSDV at 500 s −0.39 [−1.18, +0.40], at 1000 s
  +0.13 [−0.92, +1.18]). The settled readings (#4, #13) and the full data
  path (#108) did not change that.
- **Secondary.**
  - BeeAdHoc delivers more than both references at 1–500 s, and is level
    with DSDV at 1000 s.
  - Its tail delay (d_d99) is 2–3.4 s above both references at every pause
    time. That is the cost of the hold queue and the expanding ring
    (initial scout TTL 2), which AntHocNet #806 also measured.
  - Energy per delivered packet is not reported: the arm's battery levels
    read 7 throughout (#106), and the verdict line has no energy reader.

## Threats to validity

- **Version.** [PG460] ch. 7 measured an ns-2 BeeAdHoc, not the kernel module
  this repository follows (fidelity sheet, *Which version*).
- **DSDV.** The report says its DSDV comparison was thin ("left out in some
  cases due to errors", §7.1.4), and ns-3's DSDV is a different
  implementation.
- **Packet size.** 64-byte packets instead of 512 (`v270a`'s deviation)
  change the load per packet, not the packet rate.
- **Citation of record.** The citation of record, [W05] (GECCO 2005), has
  not been read. #5 re-checks the trend against it in v0.3.0. A change there
  is a new generation, never an edit of this one.

## What follows

- v0.2.0 ships with this verdict. Under org rule 6, BeeAdHoc's comparator
  numbers do not count until an anchor passes.
- #5 (v0.3.0) re-reads the anchor against [W05]. If the GECCO paper gives a
  trend or scenario that differs from §7.2.5, a new anchor is pre-registered
  under a new id.
